How do you want to play?
For grown-ups

Mental math practice for Ontario Grade 1 (2020 curriculum): recall addition facts to 10 and the related subtraction facts (B2.2), and mental strategies for adding and subtracting with numbers up to 20 (B2.3). "Make 10" and the missing-number cards practise the link between adding and subtracting (B2.1).

She types every answer herself. With 10 cards: after a wrong answer a "Show me with the abacus" button appears; after a 2nd wrong try a short narrated abacus lesson plays automatically for that exact question, then she types the answer herself. The lesson uses the soroban method: bottom beads are 1, the top bead is 5, the left rod is tens, plus the "five friends" and "ten friends" tricks when there aren't enough beads. After a 3rd wrong try the answer is shown and the card comes back at the end of the round. The very first lesson starts with a short intro to the abacus; "How does the abacus work?" on this screen replays it. Cards she misses go into her Tricky cards deck and leave it once she gets them right on the first try twice. Questions are read aloud (🗣️ button to turn off). A keyboard works too (numbers, Backspace, Enter). Progress is saved only on this device.
